Top 4 Red Flags Targeting Indian Students

payments

Upfront Fee Demand

Real companies never ask for registration, gate pass, ID card, or laptop courier money. Any fee is 100% fraud.

mail

Free Email Providers

HR recruiters always write from official corporate domains (@company.com), never public Gmail or Yahoo accounts.

chat

WhatsApp-Only Hiring

Beware offers issued entirely on messaging apps without official HR portal access or corporate landlines.

assignment_turned_in

No Interview Rounds

Instant selection without any technical screening or telephonic evaluation is typical of fee phishing syndicates.
